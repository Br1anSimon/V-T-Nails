About the salon

Family-owned, right on Liberty Ave.

V&T Nails is a small, family-run shop in Bloomfield where the same friendly faces do your nails visit after visit. Tammy, Liz and Jessica take the time to listen to what you want, then get the details right.

Regulars say they come for the work and stay for the conversation. Whether you need a quick polish change or a full set, you will be treated like one of the family.
